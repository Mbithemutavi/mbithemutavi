About

Design is a tool for equity.

I'm a brand and design consultant specialising in visual communication for social impact organisations. For the past 10+ years, I've partnered with NGOs, feminist publications, and social enterprises to create design that doesn't just look good — it moves missions forward.

My approach combines strategic thinking with hand-crafted illustration and culturally grounded visual storytelling. Whether designing a brand identity for a climate initiative, laying out a 50-page training manual, or illustrating a children's book, I bring both creative vision and project management rigor to every collaboration.

I work with organisations that prioritise community and inclusion, need design that reflects African realities, and value thoughtful, strategic creative partnership.
